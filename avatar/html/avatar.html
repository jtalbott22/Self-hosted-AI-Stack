      Your own voiceTeach it a voice from a recording

      Kokoro can learn a voice from a short clip of one person talking. Record yourself here, or choose an audio or video file. Your server uses up to 30 seconds of it. A quiet room and your normal speaking voice work best. Music or other voices in the background will make it sound worse.
